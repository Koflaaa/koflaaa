Training system

Training at bfi IT-L@B Villach follows the three-part system, which includes a combination of vocational school, practice-orientated training workshop (bfi IT-L@B) and practice-orientated partner companies in the business world.

The apprenticeship

The bfi-IT-L@B was established in 2000 at the behest of the state of Carinthia and has since proven to be a highly successful model. At three locations, young adults receive high-quality training that allows them to develop their individual strengths. The training system combines a vocational school education, supervised work placements and, as an additional third module, the bfi-IT-L@B. The theoretical foundations are followed by basic training at the bfi-IT-L@B, after which the apprentices apply their knowledge in practice. The close coordination between the company and the training centre makes training possible. The bfi-IT-L@B focuses on independent work, which is supplemented by specialist trainers from the business world. In addition to specialised knowledge, social skills such as teamwork and customer orientation are also taught.
